About Ganeshotsav

Ganeshotsav is a sacred ten-day Hindu festival honoring Lord Ganesha, the God of wisdom and prosperity. The Windows Society proudly organizes this celebration to unite our community in devotion and cultural harmony.

From August 27th to September 6th, 2025, we'll host cultural events, competitions, and spiritual activities that showcase our rich heritage and strengthen community bonds.
